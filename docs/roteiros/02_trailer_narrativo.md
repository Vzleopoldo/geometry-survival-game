## Prompts para o Google Flow

Prompts em inglês. **Enviem o print `docs/evidencias/v1.4/03_menu.png` como referência** nas cenas com Axi-01 e repitam a descrição da personagem. Não peçam textos ao gerador: coloquem-nos na edição. Gerem 2 ou 3 versões de cada.

**Estilo (colar no fim de todos):**
`2D vector neon style, glowing cyan and gold outlines, dark navy nebula, geometric minimalism, cinematic lighting, 16:9, no text, no logos.`

1. **Origem:** `A single point of light in black space draws a line that unfolds into a glowing triangle, a square and a circle, slow push-in.`
2. **Arquipélago:** `Three faceted floating islands in a starry nebula linked by golden energy paths, slow aerial camera, peaceful mood.`
3. **Fenda:** `A jagged diamond-shaped crimson rift tears open above floating islands, red lightning, energy paths fading, camera shake.`
4. **Formas Caóticas:** `Distorted glitching triangles, circles and rectangles pour out of a crimson rift over a cyan, a violet and a red floating island.`
5. **Axi-01:** `A slender geometric android, round helmet with a horizontal cyan visor, dark triangular torso, gold diamond core, floating hands, golden pentagon halo behind the head, powers on while standing on a floating platform.`
6. **Geo:** `A small dark spherical drone with one glowing eye and an orbital ring, its eye shifts from cyan to red as it hovers beside a slender geometric android.`
7. **Confronto:** `Seen from behind, a slender geometric android with a golden pentagon halo faces a colossal crimson rift, hand raised with a cyan beam charging, slow dolly in.`
